# Research Prompt: campaign-concept

**For:** `cc-concept:campaign-concept`
**Current knowledge base:** `plugins/cc-concept/skills/_shared/campaign-frameworks.md` —
SOSTAC (overall structure), RACE (channel sequencing), and Message House
(message architecture), composed together on every run. Applied from general
knowledge; unlike `audience-personas`, `competitive-research`, and
`seo-research`, there's no `docs/research/` doc backing these frameworks yet.
**Why research helps:** these three frameworks are widely taught but rarely
validated together as a composite — worth checking each individually and
checking whether composing all three is itself sound practice.

```
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Research prompt: campaign planning frameworks — SOSTAC, RACE, Message House
─────────────────────────────────────────────
You are a research analyst investigating the evidence base and current
practitioner standing of three marketing campaign-planning frameworks, for
use in an AI marketing-strategy tool that composes all three together on
every campaign it plans.

Cover, with headers separating each area:

1. SOSTAC (Situation, Objectives, Strategy, Tactics, Action, Control) —
   origin (PR Smith), current standing among marketing academics and
   practitioners, common critiques, and how it's evolved or been adapted
   since its introduction.
2. RACE (Reach, Act, Convert, Engage) — origin (Smart Insights / Dave
   Chaffey), evidence for its usefulness in sequencing channels by funnel
   stage, and how it compares to other funnel models (AIDA, the classic
   marketing funnel, jobs-to-be-done-based journey models) in current use.
3. Message House as a message-architecture framework — origin and
   prevalence in PR/comms practice, and any research on whether a
   hierarchical single-message-plus-pillars structure actually improves
   message retention or campaign coherence versus alternatives.
4. Whether composing all three frameworks together (SOSTAC for structure,
   RACE for channel sequencing, Message House for messaging) on every
   campaign is sound practice, or whether there's a case for a lighter
   composite for smaller/simpler campaigns — look for practitioner
   guidance on right-sizing campaign-planning rigor to campaign size.
5. Any current, well-evidenced alternative or complementary frameworks worth
   considering (e.g. the STP model, the 4Ps/7Ps, growth-marketing-specific
   frameworks) and where they'd fit relative to the three above.

Require credible, verifiable sources throughout, with in-text citations —
prioritize marketing-academic sources and well-established practitioner
publications (e.g. Smart Insights, CIM, published case studies) over generic
marketing-blog content. Provide a formatted source list in APA style at the
end. Respond in Markdown.
─────────────────────────────────────────────
```
